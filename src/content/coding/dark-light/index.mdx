## Overview

Dark Light is a room-looping prototype inspired by P.T. There's nothing to do but walk. The exercise was building the loop and the space it runs through.

## How it works

I handled the level design, the looping room mechanic, and a first-person camera mounted on a full-body character from Mixamo, so the view moves with the character's animation instead of floating through the hallway.
