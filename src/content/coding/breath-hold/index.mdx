## Overview

Breath Hold is a stealth mechanic for a horror game: hold your breath to stay hidden, but the longer you hold it, the harder it is to keep going. It later became the core survival mechanic in [SVTCH](/portfolio/design/svtch).

## How it works

Holding Alt starts a breath-hold timer with a configurable maximum duration. Releasing Alt, or running out of breath, ends the hold and triggers the exhale.

While the timer runs, the elapsed time is normalised to a value from 0 to 1 that drives both the post-processing vignette and the camera shake amplitude, so the pressure builds smoothly instead of in steps.

The exhale is dynamic. The hold duration is passed to the breathing audio on release and sets the intensity of the breath out, so a short hold ends in a quiet breath and a full one ends in a heavy gasp.

The prototype also includes a raycast-based interaction system for the objects in the scene.
